ABOUT US

Building trust across Avadi, one plot and one home at a time

SP Real Estate & Constructions was founded in 2010 in Kovilpathagai, Avadi, with a simple goal: help local families buy land and build homes without the confusion, delays or hidden risks that come with the property market. Sixteen years on, we're proud to be a name families in this neighbourhood recommend to their own relatives.

What began as a small plot brokerage has grown into a full real estate and construction company — we now guide clients through the entire journey, from choosing the right plot to handing over the keys to their finished home.

  • Verified, clear-title plots — every time
  • RERA compliant documentation
  • Transparent pricing, no hidden charges
  • End-to-end construction under one roof
