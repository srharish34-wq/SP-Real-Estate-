OUR STORY

From a small plot brokerage to a full-service real estate & construction company

SP Real Estate & Constructions was founded in 2010 in Kovilpathagai, Avadi, by three partners who shared one belief — that buying land or building a home shouldn't come with confusion, delay, or hidden risk.

What started as a handful of plot deals among neighbours has grown, over sixteen years, into a trusted name across Avadi and the surrounding areas. Today we guide clients through the entire journey — from choosing a verified, clear-title plot, to registration, to handing over the keys of a finished home — all under one roof, with one accountable team.

We're still a local company at heart. Our office in Kovilpathagai is open every day, and every client who walks in is treated the way we'd want our own family treated — with honesty, and without pressure.
